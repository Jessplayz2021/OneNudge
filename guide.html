Getting started

Send your first reminder in about two minutes.

On this page

  1. Before you start
  2. Send your first reminder
  3. Work through a list of students
  4. Make the wording your own
  5. Handy tips

1. Before you start

OneNudge writes reminder messages for you: an email to a student's employer, or a text to the student. You stay in control the whole time.

  • Nothing is sent automatically. OneNudge opens the message in your own email or messaging app, and you press send.
  • Nothing about students is stored. Details stay on the page while you use it and are gone when you close it.
  • Check before using real student data. Your college may have rules about which tools can be used with student information, so ask your IT or data protection team first.

2. Send your first reminder

  1. Choose the reminder

    At the top of the Reminder Dashboard, pick the reminder type (for example "Portfolio update") and the tone. Start with "Friendly nudge", then use "Firm follow-up" or "Final reminder" if it needs chasing. Add a due date if there's a deadline.

  2. Add the student's details

    You only need the fields for the message you're sending:

    FieldEmployer emailStudent SMS
    Student nameNeededNeeded
    Employer emailNeededNot used
    Employer contact nameOptional: "Dear Mark" instead of "Dear Employer"Not used
    Student mobile numberNot usedNeeded: a UK mobile, such as 07123 456789

  3. Prepare the message

    Click Prepare Employer Email or Prepare Student SMS. If anything is missing, a short note in red appears under that field.

  4. Check and tweak it

    The message appears in Message Preview. You can edit any of the wording there before it goes anywhere. For texts, the counter underneath shows how many characters you've used and whether it fits in one text.

  5. Open it and press send

    Click Open in email app or Open in Messages to open the message ready to go, then press send in your app. Prefer to paste it somewhere yourself? Use Copy message or Copy SMS text instead.

"Open in Messages" needs a device that can send texts: a phone, or a Windows laptop with Phone Link connected to your phone.

3. Work through a list of students

Got several students to chase? Batch mode saves you typing them in one by one.

  1. Copy the rows from your spreadsheet

    Arrange the columns in this order: student name, mobile number, employer email, employer contact (the last one is optional). Select the rows and copy them. Including the header row is fine. If your columns have clear headings, they can be in any order.

  2. Paste and load

    Open Batch mode on the dashboard, paste into the box and click Load list. The first student's details fill the form automatically.

  3. Send, then move on

    Prepare and send their messages as normal, then click Next →. Each student gets a tick ("Email ✓", "SMS ✓") once you've opened or copied their message, so you can see at a glance who's done.

The list is never saved. It disappears when you close or refresh the page.

4. Make the wording your own

If you find yourself editing the same thing every time, change the template instead.

  1. Open Customise templates

    It's at the bottom of the dashboard. Choose which message (employer email or student SMS) and which tone you want to change.

  2. Edit the wording

    Write it however you like. The words in curly brackets are placeholders, which OneNudge fills in for each student. For example, {studentFirst} becomes the student's first name and {due} becomes "by Friday 5 December". Click a placeholder button to insert it.

  3. Save it

    Click Save template. Your wording is used from then on, in this browser. Changed your mind? Reset to default brings the original back.

5. Handy tips

  • Moving on to the next student? Clear student details empties the student fields but keeps your reminder type, tone and due date.
  • Prefer a lighter screen? Use the ☀︎ / ☾︎ button in the top corner to switch between light and dark.
  • Want it on your phone? Open OneNudge in your phone's browser and choose Add to Home Screen (iPhone: the Share button in Safari; Android: the ⋮ menu in Chrome). It then opens like an app and works without signal.
  • On a computer, Chrome and Edge show an install icon in the address bar that does the same thing.
