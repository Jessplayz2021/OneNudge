    ✎ Customise templatesChange the wording OneNudge uses. Saved in this browser only.

    Click a placeholder to insert it. OneNudge fills these in for each student:

    • student's full name
    • student's first name
    • employer contact, or "Employer"
    • what needs doing (from the reminder type)
    • short topic, e.g. "OneFile update"
    • " by Friday 5 December", or nothing if no date
    • your full name
    • your first name

    How it works

    1. Choose a reminder

      Pick what needs doing, how firm to be, and an optional due date.

    2. Add the student

      Type their details, or paste a whole list from a spreadsheet and work through it.

    3. Check and send

      Tweak the wording if you like, then open it in your email or messaging app and press send.

    Student details never leave this page and are never stored. Only your theme choice and any custom template wording are saved, in this browser.
