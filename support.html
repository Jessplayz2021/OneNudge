First time using it? Start with the getting started guide.

Common questions

Does OneNudge send messages for me?

No. It prepares the email or text and opens it in your own email or messaging app. Nothing is sent until you press send yourself.

Is student information stored anywhere?

No. Student details stay in the page while you use it and are never saved or sent to a server. Batch lists disappear when you close the page. The only things remembered, in your browser, are your light/dark choice and any custom template wording.

"Open in Messages" doesn't do anything on my laptop

It needs an app that can send texts. It works on phones, and on Windows with Phone Link connected to your phone. Otherwise, use Copy SMS text and paste the message wherever you send texts from.

Can I change the wording of the messages?

Yes. Edit any message in the preview before you send it, or open Customise templates on the dashboard to change the default wording for each tone.

Can I use it with real student data?

Please check with your college IT or data protection team first. OneNudge doesn't store anything, but your college may have its own rules about tools used with student information.
